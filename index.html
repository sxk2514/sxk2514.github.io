01 About

I am a PhD student at Case Western Reserve University, advised by Dr. Zachary Patterson, after completing my B.Tech. in Robotics and Cyber-Physical Systems at Plaksha University. My work sits at the intersection of learning-based control and physical embodiment: taking policies from simulation and making them survive contact, delay and noise on real hardware.
